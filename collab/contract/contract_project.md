## Purpose of the collaboration

## Ambition level
We will aim to create a solid and well-developed product accompanied by an in-depth, well-written, and structured report.

## Scope

## Responsibility
Each member is responsible for completing their assigned tasks and actively contributing to the project's overall progress. Every group member must take responsibility for their individual sub-tasks and ensure that they are perfomed correctly.
It is important that if someone needs help, they ask the group for support. If the issue is too complex, help should be sough from the project supervisor.

All work must be reviewed by another group member who did not participate in creating it. The person/people reviewing the work also become the contact person for that task if it cannot be completed or if issues arise.

## Group Tone

### Tone within the group
We keep things casual and speak as we normally do in everyday life. We commit to treating each other with respect and kindness. Discussions and feedback must be constructive and delivered in a positive and supportive tone. There are no stupid questions, and asking for help is always welcome.

## Absence
If a member is unable to attend a meeting or workday, they must inform the group no later than the day before.

In special cases such as acute illness or unforeseen events, it is acceptable to be absent as long as the remaining members are informed.

## Organization and meetings

## Communication
Clear communication is essential for good group work. Therefore, the group has created a Discord server to easily contact other members. Each member is expected to regularly check Discord and respond as quickly as possible to support each other.

It is important that members are not afraid to ask questions—there are no stupid questions. A good tone must always be maintained, and personal attacks are not tolerated.

### Meetings
After supervisor meetings, we hold scrum meetings—these will usually take place on Tuesdays.

### Project work
We focus on project work early in the process to align on important decisions from the beginning.

## Decision making process
Decisions in the group are made by consensus. If consensus cannot be reached, we vote, and the majority decides. Decisions related to the work must be made during physical meetings or through Discord attendance. If you are not present at the meeting, you do not have voting rights.

Important decisions will be made via a vote in the Discord voting channel if physical attendance is impossible and a valid reason has been given in advance.


## Disagreements
In case of disagreements, we will attempt to resolve conflicts internally through dialogue. If this fails, we will involve a supervisor to help find a solution. Internal HR is responsible for managing this.

## Contract breach
If a member repeatedly fails to meet their obligations without valid reason, the group will discuss the issue. In severe or repeated cases, it may be necessary to contact the instructor or a supervisor for further action. Internal HR handles this.

KR is responsible for managing the cake arrangement, and all members contribute money to this purpose.
