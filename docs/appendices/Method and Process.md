# Method and Process
Throughout the project, the team has employed various methods and processes to ensure effective collaboration and successful project completion. This appendix outlines the key aspects of our approach.

The technical aspects of the project has been supported by various tools and methodologies, which was chosen based on their suitability for our specific needs. 
In the beginning of the project, the team brainstormed and discussed different ideas for the project, leading to the selection of a project that aligned with our ambitions and interests, as well as the requirements set forth by the course.

The ideas was discussed and evaluated based on cost, timeframe, resources, ect. but also on the team members opportunities to learn and develop new skills.
As part of this process, the technical analysis and requirements was discussed for each idea, to ensure that the team had a clear understanding of the technical challenges and opportunities associated with each option.

After selecting the project concept, the development process was initiated. The team aimed to apply agile methodologies, allowing for flexibility and adaptability throughout the project lifecycle.
The initial phase of the project was concerned with specifying the requirements for the project, as well as deciding on tools and components to be used.
A technical analysis was conducted parallel to the requirement specification, to ensure that the technical aspects of the project were aligned with the defined requirements.

During the specification of the requirements, the team utilized methods and tools learned from courses on previous semesters.
The requirements was divided into functional and non-functional requirements, providing a clear framework for the development process. The non-functional requirements was created with a basis in the FURPS+ model, to ensure a comprehensive coverage of quality attributes.
The team utilized use cases to illustrate and define the functional requirements, providing a clear understanding of the expected behavior of the system. This approach ensured that all relevant functionality for the system was included in the requirements specification.
Defining the use cases facilitated discussions and learning opportunities among team members, enhancing our collective understanding of the project goals and user needs.
The process of defining requirements was iterative, with continuous refinement and adjustment based on feedback and insights gained throughout the project. 

The team has been quite ambitious throughout the project. Therefore, a prioritization of the requirements was necessary to ensure that the most critical features were developed first. This prioritization was based on factors such as user needs, technical feasibility, and project constraints.
The prioritization was made using the MoSCoW method, categorizing requirements into Must have, Should have, Could have, and Won't have. This approach allowed the team to focus on delivering the most important features while managing scope effectively.
