# Preface
The presented report has been made by students from the Software Engineering program at Aarhus University as part of the third semester course Software Project 3.
The project titled "Self Driving Medicine Delivering System", was made in the fall semester of 2025 under the supervision of "Jenny" Jung Min Kim. The report was submitted on 12 of December 2025.

The project takes its starting point in the challenges faced by modern hospitals, particularly the increasing pressure on medical staff and the impact this has on the patients safety. Throughout the report, we examine how an automated delivery system can support existing workflows and reduce the cognitive load placed on nurses. In context, the report not only presents the technical solution we have developed but also situates the system within the broader healtcare environments, where human error, workload and resource limitations form essential parts of the problem landscape

# Reading guide
The report is structured such to guide the reader progressively from context to implementation. It begins with an introduction showcasing the motivation for the project, followed by a detailed problem formulation that defines the specific challenges the systems aims to address.
Following chapters describe the system requirements, architectural design, key technical decisions and limitations.
The implementation section provides an in depth description of the server, the car and the collective communication. This is followed by the evaluation of the system, including testing procedures on their own and together to assess its performance collective and separate.
The report concludes with a discussion of the system's limitations and potential avenues for future development of the system. 
Supporting material like diagrams, code, supplementary explanations and technical specifications can be found in the appendices and may be consulted in the main report whenever relevant
